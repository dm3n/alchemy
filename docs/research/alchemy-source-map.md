# Alchemy evidence map

## Thesis

The paper defines alchemy as the disciplined process by which an imaginal form is
embodied, enacted, and stabilized as material reality. The defensible causal chain is:

`imaginal form -> embodied state -> policy -> action -> world change -> feedback`

The paper does not claim that thought alone violates physical law. It argues that
conscious representation changes attention, valuation, physiology, identity, and
action, thereby changing the distribution of reachable outcomes.

## Evidence layers

### Established mechanisms

- Mental imagery recruits action-relevant neural systems and can improve performance
  when joined to practice.
- Implementation intentions connect situational cues to planned responses.
- Placebo and expectation research show that meaning can alter measurable experience
  and physiology within bounded conditions.
- Repetition in stable contexts produces habits, and recurring action supplies evidence
  from which people infer aspects of identity.
- Sleep loss, dehydration, inflammation, metabolic state, and persistent interruption
  can impair cognition, mood, or control.
- Bioelectric signaling is a genuine part of development and repair. This fact does not
  make every electromagnetic or quantum claim true.

### Formal results

The mediation, reachable-future, repetition, and identity-convergence results are
deductions inside the paper's stated model. They are not presented as newly discovered
laws of physics.

### Historical and interpretive synthesis

Hermetic alchemy, Jungian alchemy, kundalini traditions, Christian theosis, Christ
consciousness, and sacred-secretion narratives are read as different symbolic grammars
for disciplined transformation. Their comparison is interpretive. It is not evidence
that their cosmologies or physiological details are interchangeable.

### Documentary case studies

- U.S. Patent 6,506,148 B2 establishes that Hendricus G. Loos disclosed and received a
  patent for a proposed monitor-mediated low-frequency stimulation method. A patent
  grant is not independent experimental replication.
- The 1983 Army memorandum *Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process* establishes
  that an Army officer analyzed the Monroe Institute system, discussed hypnosis,
  biofeedback, meditation, kundalini, Hemi-Sync, patterning, and altered-state claims,
  and recommended further experimentation. CIA archival custody is not CIA authorship
  or scientific validation.

The paper treats Gateway as technological alchemy: an institutional attempt to
operationalize inner-state disciplines with audio, feedback, staged exercises, and a
technical vocabulary.

## Boundary rules

1. Symbolic similarity is not mechanistic identity.
2. Institutional attention is not institutional endorsement.
3. A patent is a legal document, not a clinical trial.
4. Quantum effects in biology do not establish macroscopic timeline selection.
5. Electromagnetic activity in the body does not establish that the body receives
   semantic messages from an external field.
6. DNA is genetic material with physical and electrical properties. Those properties
   do not establish that spoken words rewrite DNA.
7. Strong claims require controlled, reproducible evidence.
