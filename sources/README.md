# Primary documentary sources for Alchemy

These two public records are analyzed directly in *Alchemy: A Formal Theory of
Consciousness, Embodiment, and Materialization*. They are included so every documentary
claim can be audited against the exact source material.

## U.S. Patent 6,506,148 B2

- File: `US6506148.pdf`
- Title: *Nervous System Manipulation by Electromagnetic Fields from Monitors*
- Inventor: Hendricus G. Loos
- Granted: January 14, 2003
- Official record: <https://patents.google.com/patent/US6506148B2/en>
- SHA-256: `370c090ca861d7c9797af9ef11b57bbe7f7051da2bfa5a59adc88592037b360c`

The patent is evidence that a low-frequency monitor-emission method was proposed and
granted. Its specification describes observations in a subject using a subjective
eyelid-ptosis measure and subject-controlled frequency tuning. It is not a substitute
for a blinded, controlled, independently replicated multi-participant study.

## Gateway Process memorandum

- File: `cia-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5.pdf`
- Title: *Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process*
- Author: Wayne M. McDonnell
- Origin: Department of the Army, U.S. Army Operational Group
- Date: June 9, 1983
- Archive record: `CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5`
- Official archive: <https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5.pdf>
- SHA-256: `dff63304a51a547ae41141006314e67c7f100822621748b97192f64b09c6a05e`

The memorandum is evidence that a U.S. Army officer analyzed the Monroe Institute's
Gateway program and possible applications. It discusses hypnosis, biofeedback,
meditation, kundalini, Hemi-Sync, patterning, and altered-state claims. Its conclusion
proposes future experiments. CIA archival release establishes provenance and custody,
not CIA authorship, experimental confirmation, or scientific endorsement.

In the paper, Gateway is treated as a case of technological alchemy: an attempt to
translate older disciplines of attention and transformation into a staged technical
system. Within the paper's two-plane model, it attempts to condition the human bridge
that carries mental forms into physical action. That interpretation concerns the
structure of the program. It does not turn the memorandum's speculative mechanisms
into established science.

## Evidence rule

A document is primary evidence for what the document itself establishes:

- A patent establishes the disclosed claim, priority, and legal grant.
- An institutional memorandum establishes that its author and organization considered
  the subject.
- Declassification establishes release status.
- Experimental validation requires methods, controls, data, and replication.
