# Two-Plane Alchemy Revision

## Purpose

Revise *Alchemy: A Formal Theory of Consciousness, Embodiment, and
Materialization* so its ontology contains exactly two planes:

1. the mental/astral plane;
2. the physical plane.

The human being is not a third plane. The conscious and embodied human is the
alchemical bridge through which a mental form is carried from the mental/astral plane
into physical reality.

## Central Thesis

Every deliberately created reality exists first as a mental form. A mental form may be
an image, symbol, intention, identity, plan, or experienced possible future. The human
being holds that mental form in consciousness, embodies it as state and identity,
translates it into policy and action, and stabilizes its consequences in the physical
plane.

The canonical definition becomes:

> Alchemy is the disciplined process by which a mental form is embodied and enacted
> through a human being, then stabilized as physical reality.

The canonical chain becomes:

`mental form -> embodied human bridge -> policy -> action -> physical reality -> feedback`

## Ontology

### Mental/Astral Plane

The mental/astral plane contains mental forms: images, symbols, identities, intentions,
goals, counterfactuals, and models of possible realities. The two words name one
functional plane in the paper. "Mental" identifies its representational character.
"Astral" identifies its vivid, symbolic, visionary, or self-transcendent mode of
experience.

The paper may affirm the mental/astral plane as Daniel's thesis while preserving a
professional evidence boundary. The formal causal model establishes the operation of
mental representation. It does not claim that an independently existing astral
substance has been experimentally demonstrated.

### Physical Plane

The physical plane contains embodied action and observable reality: bodies, behavior,
artifacts, relationships, documents, resources, institutions, and environments.

### Human Alchemical Bridge

The human being connects the two planes. Consciousness receives or generates the
mental form. The body supplies attention, expectation, physiology, emotion, skill,
identity, and action readiness. Policy and action transmit the mental form into the
physical plane. Feedback returns evidence from the physical plane to consciousness.

Embodiment remains a necessary mediator in the mathematical and empirical model, but
it must never be described as a third plane.

## Terminology Rules

- Use "mental form" for every causal or ontological use of "form."
- Use "higher mental form," "chosen mental form," "desired mental form," and "clear
  mental form" where the adjective matters.
- The operation previously called "Form" becomes "Mental Form."
- Purely grammatical or mathematical uses such as "conditional form" do not refer to
  the ontology and remain unchanged.
- Replace "three realms" with "two planes."
- Replace "imaginal realm," "embodied realm," and "material realm" with the approved
  two-plane vocabulary.
- Use "physical reality" or "physical plane" instead of "material realm" where the
  ontology is being named.
- Describe embodied state as part of the human bridge, never as a plane.

Rename the imaginal target variable from `I_t` to mental form `M_t`. Preserve the
materialization operator:

`P(W_(t+1) | M_t,B_t,E_t) = sum_pi P(W_(t+1) | do(pi),B_t,E_t) P(pi | M_t,B_t,E_t)`

Here `B_t` is the embodied condition of the human bridge, not a plane. The mediation,
reachable-future, identity-convergence, and repetition theorems remain mathematically
unchanged except for terminology.

## Evidence Boundaries

Retain the existing scientific and documentary discipline:

- mental representation and its embodied mediators are the formal, testable pathway;
- the astral label is phenomenological and metaphysical unless separately evidenced;
- Gateway remains a documentary case of technological alchemy, not conclusive proof;
- the patent remains a granted technical claim, not independent replication;
- symbolic convergence among alchemy, kundalini, sacred secretion, and theosis does
  not make their anatomical claims identical or established.
